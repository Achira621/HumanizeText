Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is an AI Humanizer and how does it bypass AI detectors?

An AI humanizer restructures synthetic text by introducing sentence length variety (burstiness) and vocabulary entropy (perplexity). By removing formulaic transitions like "delve into" or "testament to", it ensures text reads naturally and bypasses detector filters.

How do I bypass Turnitin and GPTZero for free?

Paste your essay or article into HumanizeText, select Academic or Natural mode, and click Humanize. The resulting text is rewritten with authentic human cadence, eliminating machine token footprints.

Is HumanizeText free with no word count limit?

Yes. All core writing tools—including the AI Humanizer, Plagiarism Checker, AI Detector, and Paraphraser—are 100% free with no account creation, no subscriptions, and no paywalls.

Does HumanizeText store or upload my text to remote servers?

No. All similarity scans, local humanization, and vault storage occur 100% on your device. When choosing Cloud AI, processing runs ephemerally in Cloudflare Workers AI RAM and is discarded immediately after generation, with zero persistent storage or training usage.
