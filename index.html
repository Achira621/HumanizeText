Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is an AI Humanizer and what does it do?

An AI humanizer restructures synthetic text by introducing sentence length variety (burstiness) and vocabulary entropy (perplexity). By removing formulaic transitions like "delve into" or "testament to", it ensures text reads with authentic human cadence.

Can any tool guarantee a 100% bypass rate on AI detectors?

No tool can honestly guarantee a 100% bypass rate. AI detectors rely on probabilistic classifiers and statistical perplexity metrics that generate frequent false positives and false negatives. HumanizeText focuses on genuine clarity, rhythm, and readable prose rather than deceptive circumvention.

Is HumanizeText free with no word count limit?

Yes. All core writing tools—including the AI Humanizer, Plagiarism Checker, AI Detector, and Paraphraser—are 100% free with no account creation, no subscriptions, and no paywalls.

Does HumanizeText store or upload my text to remote servers?

No. All similarity scans, local humanization, and vault storage occur 100% on your device. When choosing Cloud AI, processing runs ephemerally in Cloudflare Workers AI RAM and is discarded immediately after generation, with zero persistent storage or training usage.
